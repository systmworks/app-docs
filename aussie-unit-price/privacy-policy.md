# Privacy policy: Aussie Unit Price

_Last updated: 30 October 2026_

## The short version

**No data is collected, stored by the developer, or sent anywhere.** Aussie Unit Price reads
product names and prices on the shop pages you visit, works out unit prices inside your browser,
and shows them on that page. The developer runs no server for this extension and receives nothing
from it.

## What the extension accesses

Only on the supported shop pages, and only to show unit prices on that page:

- **Product names and prices** shown on the page: Amazon.com.au, Bunnings, Officeworks, Chemist
  Warehouse, Dan Murphy's, BWS and Liquorland. It reads pack counts and sizes from the names, and
  prices (including multi-buy deals and member prices) from the product tiles.
- **Nothing else.** It does not read your account, cart, orders, address, postcode, sign-in or
  payment details, your browsing history, or any other website.

The extension needs the **storage** permission, and runs only on the shop addresses above.

## Where data goes

- **Nowhere.** All the work happens inside your browser. The extension makes no network requests
  of its own and has no analytics, tracking, advertising, licensing checks or error reports.
- **To no one else.** Nothing is sent to the developer or any third party. The extension loads no
  code from the internet.

## What is stored, and where

- **Toolbar settings:** your sort choice, member prices on or off, and the toolbar's position and
  collapsed state. They are kept in your own browser (Chrome extension storage) and never leave
  your computer. Removing the extension deletes them.
- Nothing else is stored.

## Changes to your accounts or the shops

None. The extension only adds labels to the page you are looking at. It never changes your cart,
orders or accounts, and it clicks only the shop's own "show more" button, when you press "Load all".

## Children

The extension is a general shopping tool. It collects no information from anyone, including
children.

## Changes to this policy

If a future version changes what is accessed or where it goes, this policy will be updated
before that version is published.
